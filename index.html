Three Pillars of Preventive Intelligence

Built for the realities of community health, not the assumptions of enterprise software.

◈
Risk Stratification
CareIQ continuously scores every patient on composite risk across chronic disease, social determinants, and utilization history. High-risk patients surface automatically so your care team acts before a crisis develops.
◎
Preventive Care Gap Identification
Real-time mapping of overdue screenings, missed immunizations, and lapsed chronic disease management across your entire patient panel. Prioritized by clinical urgency so your team closes the most critical gaps first.
⊕
Patient Health Scoring
Longitudinal health scores that track patient trajectory over time. See who is improving, who is declining, and who needs immediate outreach. Actionable intelligence, not raw data.
⬡
Data Sovereignty Protection
Patient data never leaves your infrastructure unprotected. CareIQ's Sovereign Shield layer hashes sensitive information at the edge. Built for HIPAA compliance from the architecture up, not bolted on after.
◆
FQHC Quality Measure Alignment
Built around UDS reporting requirements and HRSA quality benchmarks. CareIQ maps directly to the measures your health center is already accountable for, turning compliance reporting into a byproduct of care delivery.
◉
Community Health Intelligence
Population-level views that identify patterns across your patient panel. Social determinants mapping, neighborhood health clustering, and outreach prioritization for community health workers.
