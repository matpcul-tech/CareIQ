Data Protection
How Sovereign Shield keeps your patient data protected at every step.
⬡
Sovereign Shield Technology
CareIQ does not send raw patient data to AI systems. Every sensitive identifier is hashed at your device edge using zero-knowledge cryptography. Your patients' privacy is protected by architecture, not just policy.
How It Works
1
Data Enters the Platform
Patient records load from your EHR or manual entry. All data stays local to your device and network.
2
Shield Detects Sensitive Terms
The Sovereign Shield layer scans for names, DOBs, MRNs, diagnoses, addresses, and all PHI before any query is formed.
3
Zero-Knowledge Hashing at Edge
Sensitive terms are converted to cryptographic hashes at your device. The original values never leave your local environment.
4
Protected Query Sent to AI
Only the hashed, anonymized query reaches the CareIQ intelligence engine. Clinical insight returns without raw PHI.
5
Results Displayed Locally
AI insights map back to your local patient records. Everything stays within your controlled environment.
